A Peta Jenis Teks (Genre of Texts — Kelas 10, 11 & 12)

Sekitar 70% soal TKA berbasis teks. Hafalkan Tujuan (Purpose), Struktur, dan Ciri Kebahasaannya.

1. Descriptive Text Kelas 10

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Mendeskripsikan orang, tempat, atau benda secara spesifik (contoh: Raja Ampat, Candi Borobudur).

🧱 Struktur: Identification (pengenalan objek) $\rightarrow$ Description (ciri-ciri khusus).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Simple Present Tense, banyak Adjectives (kata sifat: breathtaking, vibrant).

2. Recount Text Kelas 10

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Menceritakan kembali pengalaman nyata atau kejadian masa lampau secara kronologis.

🧱 Struktur: Orientation (siapa, di mana, kapan) $\rightarrow$ Events (urutan kejadian) $\rightarrow$ Re-orientation (kesan penutup).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Dominan Simple Past Tense ($V_2$), kata hubung waktu (then, after that, finally).

3. Narrative Text Kelas 10

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Menghibur pembaca (to amuse/entertain) lewat kisah fiksi/dongeng dan memberi pesan moral.

🧱 Struktur: Orientation $\rightarrow$ Complication (muncul puncak masalah/konflik) $\rightarrow$ Resolution $\rightarrow$ Coda (pesan moral).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Past Tense, Action verbs, kalimat langsung (direct speech).

4. Procedure Text Kelas 10 & 12

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Menjelaskan langkah-langkah membuat atau mengoperasikan sesuatu secara berurutan.

🧱 Struktur: Goal/Aim $\rightarrow$ Materials/Tools $\rightarrow$ Steps/Methods.

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Imperative sentences (kalimat perintah: Pour, Mix, Press, Plug in), numbering/connectives.

5. Analytical Exposition Kelas 11

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Meyakinkan pembaca bahwa suatu topik penting untuk dibahas ("to persuade the reader that something is the case").

🧱 Struktur: Thesis $\rightarrow$ Arguments $\rightarrow$ Reiteration (kesimpulan ulang tanpa saran tindakan).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Simple Present, konjungsi urutan (firstly, furthermore), kausalitas.

6. Hortatory Exposition Kelas 11

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Meyakinkan pembaca bahwa sesuatu harus atau tidak boleh dilakukan ("should/should not be the case").

🧱 Struktur: Thesis $\rightarrow$ Arguments $\rightarrow$ Recommendation (saran tindakan di paragraf akhir).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Menggunakan Modals kuat (must, should, ought to) pada bagian Recommendation.

7. Explanation Text Kelas 11

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Menjelaskan proses bagaimana dan mengapa (How & Why) fenomena alam, ilmiah, atau sosial terjadi.

🧱 Struktur: General Statement $\rightarrow$ Sequenced Explanation $\rightarrow$ Closing.

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Banyak memakai Passive Voice, Simple Present, istilah teknis ilmiah.

8. Discussion Text Kelas 12

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Membahas satu isu kontroversial dari dua sudut pandang berbeda (Pro/Pendukung vs Kontra/Penentang).

🧱 Struktur: Issue $\rightarrow$ Arguments For (Proponents) $\rightarrow$ Arguments Against (Critics) $\rightarrow$ Conclusion.

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Konjungsi pertentangan (on the other hand, however, nevertheless).

9. News Item Text Kelas 12

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Menginformasikan peristiwa aktual yang penting dan layak diberitakan (newsworthy events).

🧱 Struktur: Main Event (inti berita) $\rightarrow$ Background Event / Elaboration $\rightarrow$ Source (kutipan saksi/pejabat/ahli).

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Saying verbs (announced, said, stated), nama lokasi di awal teks.

10. Review Text Kelas 12

🎯 Tujuan (Purpose): Mengevaluasi, mengulas, atau mengkritisi karya seni/produk (film, buku, album lagu).

🧱 Struktur: Orientation $\rightarrow$ Interpretative Recount $\rightarrow$ Evaluation $\rightarrow$ Evaluative Summation.

🔤 Ciri Bahasa: Kata sifat penilaian (masterpiece, fascinating, disappointing).

📌 Teks Fungsional Pendek (Short Functional Texts Kelas 10–12)

Kelas 10: Announcement & Memo

Menginformasikan jadwal/aturan resmi kepada publik/siswa. Perhatikan To (penerima), Purpose, dan Consequence jika melanggar.

Kelas 11: Invitation & Personal Letter

Undangan resmi (Formal Invitation) menggunakan bahasa baku dan mencantumkan RSVP (konfirmasi kehadiran).

Kelas 12: Application Letter & Caption

Surat Lamaran Kerja: Opening (posisi yang dilamar), Body (kualifikasi & pengalaman), Closing (lampiran CV & harapan wawancara).

B Intisari Tata Bahasa (Grammar & Structure)

Rumus cepat untuk menjawab soal nomor 1–20 tanpa terjebak pengecoh.

1. Sistem Tenses & Kunci Waktu

  • • Prediksi Bukti Fisik: Look at the dark clouds! $\rightarrow$ gunakan be going to + V1 (is going to rain).
  • • Present Perfect: $S + \text{have/has} + V_3$ (atau $\text{been} + V_{\text{ing}}$). Ingat: FOR + durasi waktu (for two hours), SINCE + titik awal waktu (since 8 o'clock).
  • • Past Continuous (Kejadian dipotong di masa lalu):
    While + Past Continuous ($\text{was/were} + V_{\text{ing}}$), Simple Past ($V_2$)
    Past Continuous + When + Simple Past ($V_2$)
  • • Past Perfect (Selesai lebih dulu di masa lalu):
    Penanda: By the time / Before + Simple Past ($V_2$) $\rightarrow$ pasangannya wajib $\text{had} + V_3$.
  • • Future Perfect (Akan sudah selesai di masa depan):
    Penanda: By + waktu masa depan (By the end of this year) $\rightarrow$ wajib $\text{will have} + V_3$.

2. Passive Voice (Kalimat Pasif: $\text{Be} + V_3$)

Jika subjek adalah benda yang dikenai pekerjaan, ubah ke bentuk pasif:

🔹 Simple Present: $\text{is / am / are} + V_3$

🔹 Present / Past Continuous (right now): $\text{is/am/are/was/were} + \text{being} + V_3$ (contoh: is being built)

🔹 Simple Past: $\text{was / were} + V_3$

🔹 Perfect Tense: $\text{have / has / had} + \text{been} + V_3$

🔹 Setelah Modals (must, can, will, should): $\text{Modal} + \text{be} + V_3$ (contoh: must be followed)

3. Conditional Sentences & Inversi (Tanpa "If")

Type 1 (Mungkin terjadi): $\text{If} + V_1 \rightarrow \text{will/can} + V_1$
Inversi: Should + $S + V_1$
Type 2 (Tidak nyata sekarang / Present Unreal):
Rumus: $\text{If} + V_2 (\text{had/were}) \rightarrow \text{would/could} + V_1$
Fakta: Dalam Simple Present & berlawanan sifat. Inversi: Were + $S$ ...
Type 3 (Penyesalan masa lalu / Past Unreal):
Rumus: $\text{If} + \text{had } V_3 \rightarrow \text{would/could have} + V_3$
Fakta: Dalam Simple Past ($V_2$) & berlawanan (Positif $\leftrightarrow$ Negatif).
Inversi: Had + $S + V_3$ (contoh: "Had I known..." = "If I had known...").

4. Relative Clauses & Conjunctions

  • • WHO + Verb (Subjek orang) | WHOM + Subjek + Verb (Objek orang: The candidates whom the committee interviewed).
  • • WHOSE + Noun (Kepemilikan: The scientist whose research won...).
  • • Pertentangan (Contrast):
    - Diikuti Kalimat ($S+V$): Although, Even though, Though.
    - Diikuti Frasa Benda (Noun Phrase): Despite / In spite of (Contoh: Despite the heavy rain... — jangan pakai of setelah despite!).
  • • Sebab-Akibat (Cause-Effect):
    - Diikuti Kalimat ($S+V$): Because, Since, As.
    - Diikuti Frasa Benda (Noun Phrase): due to / because of / owing to (Contoh: ...due to the thick fog).

5. Causative Verbs, Agreement, Reported Speech & Gerund

🛠️ Causative Verbs (Menyuruh)

• Aktif (Objek Orang):
$\text{Have/Make} + \text{Orang} + \mathbf{V_1}$ (had the technician fix)
$\text{Get} + \text{Orang} + \mathbf{\text{to } V_1}$

• Pasif (Objek Benda):
$\text{Have/Get} + \text{Benda} + \mathbf{V_3}$ (got her picture taken)

🗣️ Reported Speech & Agreement

• Neither... nor... / Either... or...: Verb mengikuti subjek terdekat/belakang (Neither the manager nor the employees were...).

• Indirect Speech: Jika pengantar said, maka $V_2$ (submitted) mundur jadi $\text{had } V_3$ (had submitted), yesterday $\rightarrow$ the day before.

💡 Modals & Gerund ($V_{\text{ing}}$)

• Had better + $V_1$: Saran kuat/mendesak (You had better take a rest).

• Kata Kerja + Gerund ($V_{\text{ing}}$): Kata postpone, avoid, consider, finish, mind wajib diikuti $V_{\text{ing}}$ (postpone launching).

C Ungkapan Fungsional & Strategi Reading TKA

💬 Functional Expressions (Kelas 10–12)

  • • Congratulating: "Congratulations, I'm so proud of you!" (saat seseorang lulus seleksi/meraih prestasi).
  • • Giving Suggestions:
    - Diikuti $V_{\text{ing}}$: How about / What about + $V_{\text{ing}}$? ("How about holding a brief evaluation?")
    - Diikuti $V_1$ murni: Why don't we + $V_1$ / Let's + $V_1$.
  • • Polite Disagreement (Tidak setuju secara sopan):
    "I'm afraid I have to disagree with you." (Hati-hati: "I couldn't agree more" artinya Sangat Setuju!).

🎯 Kamus Kilat & Trik Reading TKA

  • • Author's Tone: Sikap penulis terhadap topik. Jika membahas krisis kesehatan/lingkungan dan mendesak solusi $\rightarrow$ Urgent and concerned.
  • • Kosakata Langganan Kuis:
    - Breathtaking = Magnificent (Sangat memukau)
    - Shoddy = Poor-quality (Kualitas buruk/asal-asalan)
    - Retain = Keep / Hold (Menahan/menyimpan)
    - Proponents = Supporters / Advocates (Pendukung)
    - Deployed = Put into use / Implemented (Diterapkan/diterjunkan)

Contekan Rumus & Materi Cepat (Cheat Sheet)

⚡ Rumus Cepat Tenses & Passive Voice

• Bukti fisik sekarang (dark clouds) $\rightarrow$ is/am/are going to + V1.

• While + Past Continuous (was/were V-ing), Simple Past (V2).

• By the time + V2 $\rightarrow$ Past Perfect (had + V3). | By + waktu depan $\rightarrow$ Future Perfect (will have + V3).

• Pasif Continuous (right now): is/am/are + being + V3. | Pasif Modal: must/can/will + be + V3.

🔁 Conditional, Inversi, Relative Clause & Causative

• Conditional Type 2: If + V2 (had/were), would + V1. | Type 3: If + had V3, would have + V3 (Faktanya berlawanan dalam Simple Past). Inversi Type 3: Had + S + V3.

• Relative: Who + Verb | Whom + S + Verb | Whose + Noun (Kepemilikan).

• Conjunction + Noun Phrase (tanpa kata kerja): Despite / In spite of (pertentangan) & due to / because of (sebab-akibat).

• Causative: Aktif Orang = had + orang + V1 murni (fix). Pasif Benda = got + benda + V3 (taken).

📑 Ciri 10 Jenis Teks (Genre)

• Descriptive: Deskripsi tempat spesifik (Raja Ampat). | Recount: Pengalaman masa lalu (Simple Past). | Narrative: Cerita dengan pesan moral.

• Hortatory Exposition: Diakhiri Recommendation (must/should). | Explanation: Proses terjadinya fenomena (Urban Heat Island).

• Discussion: Pro (Proponents) vs Kontra (Critics). | News Item: Berita aktual + kutipan narasumber (Source).
